# NexAgri — AI-Powered Smart Agricultural Market Platform

**Empowering farmers with data-driven decisions, smarter market connections, and better selling opportunities.**

NexAgri is a Smart India Hackathon (SIH) project designed to help farmers make informed agricultural marketing decisions. By combining agricultural market data, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and smart market matching, NexAgri aims to help farmers identify suitable selling opportunities and evaluate their potential returns.

## Problem Statement

Farmers often face challenges when deciding where and when to sell their agricultural produce. Market prices fluctuate, access to reliable market information can be limited, and choosing the most profitable selling opportunity can be difficult.

These challenges can lead to poor selling decisions, reduced bargaining power, and missed market opportunities.

## Our Solution

NexAgri provides an intelligent agricultural market platform that analyzes relevant market information and farmer requirements to support smarter selling decisions.

The platform aims to help farmers:

- Access relevant agricultural market prices and information.
- Analyze market trends and potential price movements.
- Compare selling opportunities based on estimated net returns.
- Identify suitable markets and potential buyers.
- Make data-driven agricultural marketing decisions.
- Track transaction progress through a structured workflow.

## Key Features

### Agricultural Market Intelligence
Uses agricultural market data, including mandi prices and arrivals, to provide useful market insights.

### AI-Powered Analysis
Applies machine learning techniques to support crop-price prediction, demand forecasting, and market trend analysis.

### Net-Return Analysis
Helps compare selling opportunities by considering relevant costs and estimated revenue, rather than relying only on the listed market price.

### Smart Market Matching
Aims to match farmers' crop details and selling requirements with suitable market opportunities.

### Location-Based Market Discovery
Uses interactive maps and location information to help users explore relevant market locations.

### Transaction Workflow and Tracking
Supports a structured workflow for managing selling opportunities and monitoring transaction progress.

## System Architecture

The proposed system follows a modular architecture:

1. **Farmer Input Layer:** Collects crop details, quantity, location, and selling preferences.
2. **Market Data Layer:** Retrieves and processes relevant agricultural market information.
3. **AI Analysis Layer:** Analyzes market data and generates predictions or recommendations where supported by available data.
4. **Decision Support Layer:** Evaluates potential selling opportunities and estimated net returns.
5. **Market Matching Layer:** Identifies relevant market opportunities based on farmer requirements.
6. **Transaction Layer:** Supports the selling workflow and transaction tracking.

## How It Works

1. The farmer provides crop details and selling requirements.
2. NexAgri retrieves relevant agricultural market information.
3. The system analyzes market conditions and available opportunities.
4. Potential selling options are evaluated using estimated returns and relevant factors.
5. The farmer reviews the recommendations and chooses a suitable opportunity.
6. The transaction workflow supports subsequent tracking.
